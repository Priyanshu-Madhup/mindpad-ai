MINDPAD AI — COMPLETE FEATURE REFERENCE
========================================

## What is Mindpad AI?
Mindpad AI is an intelligent research workspace designed for students, scholars, researchers, and lifelong learners. It combines a notebook-style interface with a powerful AI assistant (Midy AI) that can answer questions, summarise documents, generate images, conduct deep web research, and import Notion pages — all within a single, beautiful UI.

---

## CORE FEATURES

### 1. Notebooks
- Create unlimited notebooks to organise research topics, projects, or subjects.
- Each notebook has its own persistent AI chat history that survives page refreshes.
- Notebooks can be renamed by double-clicking the name.
- Deleting a notebook permanently removes all associated PDFs, imported content, and chat history.

### 2. AI Chat — Midy AI
- A fast, streaming AI assistant that responds in clean formatted output (headings, tables, code blocks, lists).
- Your full conversation history is saved per notebook — returning later restores the complete chat.
- Attach images directly in the chat and ask questions about them.
- Click the speaker icon on any AI message to hear it read aloud.
- Copy any message with a single click.

### 3. Voice Input (Speech-to-Text)
- Record voice messages directly in the chat using your microphone.
- AI-powered speech recognition transcribes your words accurately and quickly.
- Animated recording UI with live "Listening…" feedback.
- Transcribed text is inserted into the chat input automatically.

### 4. PDF Upload & AI Knowledge Base
- Upload one or more PDF documents per notebook via the sidebar or the chat bar attach button.
- The AI automatically reads and understands each PDF, generates a summary, and suggests a notebook name.
- Smart deduplication: re-uploading the same PDF is instant — no redundant processing.
- Select multiple PDFs and the AI answers from all of them at once.
- PDF info (page count, size) is visible via an info icon tooltip.

### 5. Notion Integration
- Connect your Notion account and import any Notion page directly into a notebook.
- Imported pages are processed so the AI can answer questions about them — just like a PDF.
- An AI-generated summary is produced for each imported page.

### 6. Web Search
- Toggle "Web Search" in the chat input bar to pull in live search results before answering.
- Midy AI answers with up-to-date information and cites the sources it used.

### 7. Deep Research Mode
- Toggle "Deep Research" for a thorough, multi-step research process on any topic.
- Mindpad AI searches the web, reads the top pages in full, and then answers from that content.
- If PDFs are also selected, the AI combines insights from both your documents and the live web.
- A four-phase loading animation shows progress while research runs (typically 20–60 seconds).

### 8. AI Image Generation
- Ask Midy AI to generate an image using natural language ("draw me…", "generate an image of…").
- Images are detected automatically — no special command needed.
- Generated images are saved permanently and are accessible across sessions and devices.
- Copy or download generated images directly from the chat.

### 9. Text-to-Speech
- Click the speaker icon on any AI message to hear it read aloud.
- Audio plays directly in the browser with a stop button shown while playing.

### 10. Research Mode
- Activate in Settings to switch to a more powerful AI model for complex tasks.
- Produces longer, more analytical responses with cited sources, structured sections, and comparison tables.
- Best for in-depth academic analysis, multi-step reasoning, and long-form synthesis.

### 11. Language Selection
- Choose from 12 languages: English, Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Urdu, Odia.
- The AI responds entirely in the selected language, including all headings and prose.
- Your language preference is saved and restored on every visit.

### 12. Settings Panel
- Toggle Dark Mode, Black (OLED) Mode, or Light Mode.
- Enable/disable Research Mode (deeper, more analytical AI responses).
- Enable/disable Deep Research (full web reading pipeline).
- All settings are saved and restored on every visit.

### 13. Preferences Panel
- Opened via the sliders icon in the top navbar.
- Contains theme toggles, language selection, AI behaviour settings, and the Storage Usage Dashboard.

### 14. Storage Usage Dashboard
- See your total storage usage (uploaded PDFs + Insight Canvas images) against a 200 MB limit.
- A progress bar shows how much of your allowance is used, broken down by category.
- Per-file list with name, size, and a one-click permanent delete button.
- Deletion is permanent and removes the file from all systems.
- Warning shown when usage exceeds 80% or 90% of the limit.

### 15. Mind Map Generator
- Generates an interactive visual mind map from your selected PDF documents.
- Nodes can be expanded or collapsed by clicking; the layout is left-to-right.
- Supports pan (drag), scroll-to-zoom, pinch-to-zoom on mobile, and fullscreen.
- Mind map is saved per-notebook and persists across sessions.

### 16. Insight Canvas
- Generates an infographic or visual summary from selected PDFs.
- Saved per-notebook and shown in the AI Studio panel when you reopen the notebook.

### 17. AI Studio (Right Panel)
- Accessible via the panel icon in the header (slide-in drawer on mobile).
- Currently houses Mind Map and Insight Canvas. Coming soon: Audio Podcast, Visual Podcast, Video Suggestions, Flashcards, Quiz Mode.

### 18. Notifications
- Admins can push in-app announcements visible to all users via a bell icon with an unread badge.
- Notifications are dismissible.

### 19. Welcome Email
- New users receive a welcome email the first time they open a notebook. Sent only once.

### 20. Mobile Support
- Fully responsive layout optimised for phones and tablets.
- Sidebar opens as a slide-in drawer via the hamburger menu.
- AI Studio panel opens as a mobile drawer via the panel icon.
- Web Search and Deep Research collapse into a + button on mobile to save input bar space.
- Language selector always visible on mobile.

## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: Is my data private?
A: Yes. Your PDFs, Notion pages, and chat history are all tied to your account and never shared with other users.

Q: What happens to my data if I delete my account?
A: All your notebooks, uploaded PDFs, Notion content, and chat history are permanently deleted from our systems.

Q: How long does Deep Research take?
A: Typically 20–60 seconds. A four-phase loading animation shows progress while it searches and processes web content.

Q: Can I use multiple PDFs at once?
A: Yes. Select any number of PDFs in your notebook sidebar and the AI will answer from all of them at once.

Q: Does the AI remember my past conversations?
A: Yes. Each notebook has its own persistent chat history. Refreshing the page or returning later restores the full conversation.

Q: What languages can the AI respond in?
A: English, Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Urdu, and Odia (12 languages total).

Q: Can I import from Notion?
A: Yes. Connect your Notion account and import any page directly into a notebook. The content becomes searchable and the AI can answer questions about it, just like a PDF.

Q: Is there a mobile app?
A: Not yet. The web app is fully responsive and works great on phones and tablets.

Q: What is the storage limit?
A: There is a 200 MB cap for Firebase Storage (PDFs and Insight Canvas images combined). You can view your usage and delete files in the Preferences panel (Storage Usage Dashboard).

Q: What is Research Mode?
A: Research Mode switches the AI to a more powerful model (openai/gpt-oss-120b) and increases document retrieval depth. It produces longer, more analytical responses with cited sources. Toggle it in the Settings panel.
